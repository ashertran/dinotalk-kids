# Chicken Talk — Product Vision

**Product Name:** Chicken Talk  
**Tagline:** *Play Together. Talk Together. Grow Together.*  
**Mascot:** **Chicky** — a small, curious golden-yellow baby chicken explorer.  
**Target Audience:** Children aged **6–10** learning English as a second/foreign language, supported by their parents.

---

## 1. The Problem

Most English apps for children treat language as a solitary drill: tapping flashcards, filling grammar blanks, chasing stressful timers, and competing on leaderboards. Children memorize isolated words yet hesitate to speak English with real people because they rarely experience English as a meaningful tool for play, teamwork, and friendship.

## 2. Core Philosophy

Children do not open **Chicken Talk** to "study English." They enter **Chicky's Little World** to play with an approved friend, explore enchanting places, solve cooperative puzzles, and communicate naturally in English.

English becomes the **bridge** that helps two friends accomplish a shared goal.

## 3. Five Product Pillars

1. **Trusted Child-to-Child Play**: Private, parent-approved rooms only — zero strangers, zero public feeds, zero camera/video exposure.
2. **Chicky AI Guide**: A patient, playful baby chicken facilitator who models comprehensible input, uses gentle recasting, and offers adaptive hints only when needed.
3. **Cooperative Story Mini-Games**: Built around the **Information Gap** mechanic so each child holds half of the puzzle and genuinely needs to talk to their partner.
4. **Adaptive Scaffolding & Multi-Modal Play**: Supports Push-to-Talk voice, picture action cards, and 3-level sentence hints so imperfect speech recognition never blocks fun.
5. **Parent-Controlled Safety & Clarity**: Verified adult onboarding, consent preferences, approved buddy lists, session controls, and calm, non-competitive learning summaries.

## 4. What Chicken Talk Deliberately Avoids

- Conventional vocabulary quizzes and rote grammar worksheets
- Stressful countdowns, punitive error sounds, or competitive leaderboards
- Stranger matchmaking, public chat, cameras, or in-app purchases in Kids Space
- Unrestricted AI chatbot behavior pretending to be a human child

## 5. Long-Term World Roadmap

- **World 1 — The Magic Garden (MVP Complete):** Find Milo, the missing puppy (`Where is...?`, `I see...`, `Go left/right`, `Behind the tree`, `Let's go!`).
- **World 2 — My Little Home (Planned Extension):** Cooperate to decorate a cozy house (`I like...`, `Can you help me?`, `Put it here`, `What color?`, `Let's build together`).
- **World 3 — Happy Food Market (Planned Extension):** Role-play shopping and preparing a party (`Can I have...?`, `How many?`, `Here you go`, `Thank you`, `I want...`).
