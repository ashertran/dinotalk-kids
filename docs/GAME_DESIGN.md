# Chicken Talk — Game Design Specification

## 1. Chicky's Little World

| World | Theme | Status | Target Language | Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **World 1: The Magic Garden** | Find Milo, the missing puppy | **Playable (MVP)** | `Where is...?`, `I see...`, `Go left/right`, `Behind the tree`, `Let's go!` | 10–15 min |
| **World 2: My Little Home** | Decorate a cozy house together | Planned Extension | `I like...`, `Can you help me?`, `Put it here`, `What color?`, `Let's build together` | 10–15 min |
| **World 3: Happy Food Market** | Shop and prepare a party | Planned Extension | `Can I have...?`, `How many?`, `Here you go`, `Thank you`, `I want...` | 10–15 min |

## 2. Core Mechanic: Information Gap

To create an authentic reason to communicate in English, the two explorers receive **complementary information**:

- **Role A — Map Scout (`🗺️`):** Sees the interactive Magic Garden map, paths, and clickable landmarks, but cannot see Milo's tiny hidden clues.
- **Role B — Clue Spotter (`🔎`):** Sees Milo's glowing paw-print trail, collar clues, and sound hints, but needs the Map Scout to steer and check the right landmark.
- In Demo Mode, the child can pick either role (and developers/parents can preview the partner's clue card transparently), while the clearly labeled **Simulated Buddy (Luna 🐰)** plays the complementary role.

## 3. World 1: The Missing Puppy — 4-Stage Mission Script

### Intro: Chicky's Mission Briefing
Chicky greets the child and Luna, explains that playful puppy Milo chased a golden butterfly into the Magic Garden, and invites both friends to team up.

### Stage 1: The Garden Gate — Choose the Path
- **Information Gap:** Map Scout sees two garden paths (Left → Rainbow Meadow, Right → Rocky Hill). Clue Spotter sees fresh muddy paw prints leading **left**.
- **Target Phrases:** `"Where is Milo?"`, `"Go left!"`, `"Let's go!"`
- **Action:** Communicate direction & tap the Left Path sign to walk together into the meadow.

### Stage 2: The Rainbow Flower Bush — Spot the Clue
- **Information Gap:** Clue Spotter sees a **blue puppy collar** sparkling near the **tall oak tree**; Map Scout must ask what the partner sees and inspect the Rainbow Bush / Big Tree landmarks.
- **Target Phrases:** `"I see a blue collar!"`, `"Where is Milo?"`, `"Look near the tree!"`
- **Action:** Exchange visual clues and tap the sparkling clue on the scene.

### Stage 3: The Whispering Oak — Look Behind the Tree
- **Information Gap:** Milo's favorite toy bone is tucked **behind the big oak tree**, and paw prints continue **right** across the wooden bridge to the Cozy Gazebo.
- **Target Phrases:** `"Behind the tree!"`, `"Go right!"`, `"Let's go!"`
- **Action:** Tell partner to check behind the tree, collect the toy bone clue, then cross the wooden bridge together.

### Stage 4: The Cozy Gazebo — Rescue Milo!
- **Information Gap:** Soft happy barking comes from behind the berry bush next to the gazebo. Both players cheer and call out together!
- **Target Phrases:** `"I see Milo!"`, `"He is behind the bush!"`, `"Let's go!"`
- **Action:** Spot Milo peeking out, tap to welcome him, and trigger the shared celebration!

## 4. Adaptive Scaffolding (Levels 0–3) & Gentle Recasting

| Level | Support Provided | Example (Stage 3) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 0** | Visual hotspot glow + sound cue + Chicky pointing animation | Tree sparkles; Chicky points gently |
| **Level 1** | Full tappable English phrases with audio preview | `"Look behind the tree!"`, `"Go right!"`, `"Let's go!"` |
| **Level 2** | Partial sentence frames with visual word chips | `"Look ___ the tree!"` + `[behind]` |
| **Level 3** | Open natural speaking with minimal prompt | Chicky listens and responds naturally |

### Gentle Recasting Rule
Never display red "X" marks or "Wrong answer!" messages.
- Child says: `"dog tree!"` → Chicky replies: *"Ooh, is the puppy behind the tree? Let's look behind the tree together!"*
- Child says: `"go left puppy"` → Chicky replies: *"Great idea! Let's go left to find Milo!"*

## 5. Rewards & Closure

- No numeric ranking or competitive scoreboard.
- Completing the mission awards a shared cooperative badge (**🐶 Puppy Rescuers**), **Adventure Stars**, a friendly spoken phrase recap, and Chicky's waving goodbye animation.
